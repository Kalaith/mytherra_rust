What I built

A reverse gateway — the better architecture you pointed to. Everyone hits one public https URL on webhatchery.au; only the gateway talks to your PC. That kills the browser mixed-content block, hides your home IP, and centralizes auth — and it's reusable for any home service, not just Mytherra.

apps/local_gateway/ — a small PHP reverse proxy (no DB, no framework):
- POST /register (admin-token) — your box advertises its address; send {"port":8791} and it uses your public IP automatically.
- GET /services (admin) — list services + freshness.
- ANY /p/<service>/<path> — proxies through to the registered home box via curl.
- Split policy (config/services.json, deployed) vs. on, written at runtime, both blocked from directHTTP). The register token can move an address but never widen access.

Client wiring (mytherra repo, committed fc91ccc): game_config.json gained gateway_url; a published build (WebGL / release native)
targets the gateway, debug cargo run stays on localhoverrides on native. Plus register-server.ps1 toheartbeat your box's address, and the RUNNING.md flow.

Verified: health, 401/502 gating, register (explicit + public-IP-from-port), GET/POST proxying with path/query/headers/body intact, 403
on the policy/store files, and the full chain throug upstream — including that the store is writableunder Apache.
